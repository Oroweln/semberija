# Regional platform images

Approved logos and screenshots for the homepage "Povezujemo regije" carousel.
Files here are picked up automatically by `src/lib/regionalPlatforms.ts`.

Naming: `<id>-logo.<ext>` and `<id>-screenshot.<ext>`

| Platform      | Logo                      | Screenshot                       |
|---------------|---------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Lombardia.app | `lombardia-logo.svg/png`  | `lombardia-screenshot.webp/jpg`  |
| Veneto.app    | `veneto-logo.svg/png`     | `veneto-screenshot.webp/jpg`     |

Screenshots look best at a 16:10 ratio, about 1600×1000 px.
Logos should work on a white background (they sit in a white badge).
